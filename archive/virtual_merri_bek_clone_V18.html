Explore Merri-bek in 3D

Welcome to Virtual Merri-bek, an interactive 3D view of the municipality. Explore buildings and places, switch spatial data layers on and off, and use the tools to inspect the urban environment.

ExploreNavigate the 3D scene and view buildings in context.
InspectUse layer controls, sunlight and shadows, and 3D measurements.
UnderstandView available spatial data to support community understanding of the built environment.

This is a visualisation tool. Data availability and accuracy vary by layer; use official Council information for formal planning or property decisions.

Help & contact

Use the control panel to manage scene layers, change LiDAR display settings, adjust the camera, and access sunlight, shadow and measurement tools.

  • Navigate: drag to orbit, use the navigation controls, or select Home view to return to the starting camera.
  • Layers: open the Layers tab to show or hide available 3D data.
  • Sunlight & shadows: open Tools and use the Daylight widget to choose a date and time.
  • Screenshots: Screenshot scene saves the 3D view; Capture window requests permission to capture the browser window including controls.

For questions about Virtual Merri-bek or the underlying Council data, contact Merri-bek City Council through its official website.

Street-level camera

When active, click the scene to capture mouse look, then use WASD to move. Mouse movement looks around while the camera stays at the selected height above the ground. Press Esc to turn street-level mode off and return to normal 3D navigation.

Sunlight & shadows

This is the basic ArcGIS Daylight tool. Set a date and time in the widget and turn on direct shadows to see real-time sunlight and shadows on supported 3D objects. Terrain does not cast shadows. Daylight controls the scene lighting; ArcGIS does not expose shadow colour or opacity settings for this basic tool.

Use the date and time controls to adjust sunlight and enable shadows. Shadow colour and opacity are not adjustable in the basic Daylight tool.
Height above DTM

Click the button, move over a LiDAR point or building roof to preview its height, then click to pin the location and keep the measurement. Click the button again to place another pin.

About this viewer

A custom ArcGIS Maps SDK for JavaScript viewer designed to reproduce common Experience Builder workflows. It loads the public ArcGIS item configured in this application.
